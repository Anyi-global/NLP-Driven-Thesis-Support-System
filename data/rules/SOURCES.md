# Compliance rule sources

1. **UNIZIK Manual of Style (2019)**, School of Postgraduate Studies, Nnamdi Azikiwe University. Used for preliminary-page ordering, title/abstract guidance, general chapter guidance, in-text citation and reference-list summaries.
2. **Department of Computer Science, Nnamdi Azikiwe University — Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) and Master of Science (MSc) Degree in Computer Science: Documentation Format** (uploaded two-page departmental guide). Used for A4, Times New Roman, 12-point, 1.5 spacing, 1-inch margins and chapter outlines.

The JSON rules intentionally identify themselves as automated checks or heuristic flags. Do not describe results as an official university certification. PDF layout analysis is limited; use DOCX for more reliable metadata checks. Optional subsections are not all mandatory for every project; human review remains required.


## Precedence (confirmed by the project owner)
- **UNIZIK Manual of Style 2019** governs everything: preliminary pages, Chapter One and Chapter Two structure, formatting (Times New Roman 12, **double / 2.0 line spacing**, A4, **left margin 2.5 cm and right/top/bottom 2.0 cm**, page numbers bottom right with Roman then Arabic numerals), headings, tables and figures, citation and referencing.
- **Computer Science MSc/PhD Documentation Format** governs only the **section structure of Chapters Three, Four and Five**. Its other settings (A4, 1.5 spacing, 1-inch margins, Chapter One/Two lists, preliminary list) are NOT applied.

Page references are the Manual's printed page numbers (Section A pp. 1-4, B pp. 5-14, C pp. 15-34, D pp. 35-41).

Notes on the Manual:
- It states A4 as 8.27 x 11.67 in (A4 is 11.69 in); the check uses 8.27 x 11.69 with 0.1 in tolerance.
- Its Appendix A4 sample contents page numbers one entry "2.1.1.7" where "1.7" is evidently intended; treated as a typo.
- Chapter One may combine Purpose and Objectives under one heading only for the Faculties of Arts and Education. **Exception for this project:** the supervisor has approved a combined 'Aim and Objectives of the Study' heading, so the Chapter One check accepts it.
- The Manual's Chapter Three-Five descriptions are kept in the retrieval corpus as content guidance only; their section structure is superseded by the department format for Computer Science.
